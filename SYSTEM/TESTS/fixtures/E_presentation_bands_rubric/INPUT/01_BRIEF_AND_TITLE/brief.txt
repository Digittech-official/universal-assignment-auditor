NORTHBRIDGE UNIVERSITY - DEPARTMENT OF ENVIRONMENTAL SCIENCE
ENV3010 Sustainability in Practice
Assessment 1: Individual Presentation

TASK
Prepare a 10-minute presentation proposing a sustainability initiative for a university campus. Your audience is the University Estates Committee, who must decide whether to support the initiative.

REQUIREMENTS
- Length: 10 minutes; 8 to 12 slides.
- The presentation must include:
  1. A problem statement
  2. Supporting evidence for the problem
  3. The proposal (your sustainability initiative)
  4. A cost estimate
  5. An implementation timeline
  6. Recommendations
  7. A references slide
- Speaker notes must be provided on every content slide (all slides except the title slide and the references slide).

SUBMISSION
Submit your slide deck as a .pptx file, including speaker notes, via the VLE.

ASSESSMENT
Feedback will be given using the ENV3010 presentation rubric.
