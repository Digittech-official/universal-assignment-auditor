# ENV3010 Presentation Feedback Rubric

This rubric provides qualitative feedback levels only; no numerical marks are awarded.

| Criterion | Excellent | Good | Satisfactory | Poor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Content | All required elements are present and fully developed; the proposal is realistic, specific and clearly addresses the problem. | All required elements are present; most are well developed, with minor gaps in detail. | Most required elements are present but some are thin or one is missing. | Several required elements are missing or the proposal is unclear. |
| Structure and narrative | The presentation follows a clear, persuasive sequence from problem to recommendation, fitting the time limit; speaker notes support every content slide. | The sequence is logical with minor jumps; speaker notes are provided for almost all content slides. | The sequence is sometimes hard to follow; speaker notes are incomplete. | There is no clear structure; speaker notes are largely absent. |
| Use of evidence | Claims are consistently supported by relevant, credible and correctly cited evidence. | Most claims are supported by relevant, cited evidence. | Evidence is limited or not always clearly linked to claims. | Little or no evidence; sources are missing or not cited. |
| Visual clarity | Slides are uncluttered, readable and use visuals purposefully; text is concise. | Slides are generally clear with occasional clutter or dense text. | Slides are often text-heavy or inconsistent in layout. | Slides are difficult to read or confusing. |
