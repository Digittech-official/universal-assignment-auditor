Module HRM5003: Human Resource Management in Practice
Assessment 1: Individual Essay - Assessment Brief
Academic year 2025/26

ESSAY QUESTION
"Critically discuss whether performance-related pay improves employee motivation."

PURPOSE
This essay assesses your ability to apply motivation theory to a contemporary reward practice, to evaluate academic evidence critically and to construct a reasoned argument.

REQUIREMENTS
1. Length: the essay must be 1,000 words (+/-10%), excluding the reference list.
2. Referencing: use APA 7th edition referencing for all in-text citations and the reference list.
3. Sources: use at least 5 academic sources (peer-reviewed journal articles or scholarly books).
4. Structure: the essay must include:
   a) an introduction that sets out the question and your line of argument;
   b) a main body that critically applies at least two theoretical perspectives on motivation
      (for example, expectancy theory and self-determination theory) to performance-related pay;
   c) a conclusion that answers the essay question.
5. Format: submit the essay as a Word document (.docx) via the module VLE.

MARKING
Work will be assessed on the quality of argument, use of theory, critical evaluation of evidence, structure and referencing. Written feedback will be provided within 15 working days.
