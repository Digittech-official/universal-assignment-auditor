# MKT3002 Marketing Planning – Marking Rubric

Total: 100 marks.

| Criterion | Marks available | Descriptor |
|---|---|---|
| Situation analysis | 30 | Thorough, evidence-based analysis of the market using at least one recognised framework; clear identification of key opportunities and threats. |
| Segmentation & targeting | 25 | Well-justified choice of a primary target segment, supported by data; clear and credible positioning statement. |
| Marketing mix | 30 | Coherent and realistic 4Ps plan, with each element clearly linked to the target segment and positioning. |
| Presentation | 15 | Professional report structure, clear writing, word count respected, and accurate Harvard referencing. |
| **Total** | **100** | |
