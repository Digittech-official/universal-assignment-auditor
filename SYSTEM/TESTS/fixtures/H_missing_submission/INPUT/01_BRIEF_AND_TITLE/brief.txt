MKT3002 Marketing Planning - Individual Report Brief

Task
Write a 2,000-word marketing plan for a fictional plant-based snack brand of your own invention, preparing it for launch in the UK retail market.

Requirements
1. Length: 2,000 words (+/-10%), excluding tables, appendices and the reference list.
2. Include a situation analysis of the UK plant-based snack market using at least one recognised framework (e.g. PESTLE, Porter's Five Forces or SWOT).
3. Identify and justify a primary target segment, and write a positioning statement for the brand.
4. Set out a marketing mix (4Ps) for the launch, with a clear rationale linked to the target segment.
5. Use Harvard referencing throughout, drawing on at least 8 credible sources.

Submission
Submit a single Word or PDF document via the module VLE by 12:00 noon on the published deadline. The report will be marked against the MKT3002 rubric.
