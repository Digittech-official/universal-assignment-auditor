Northbridge University - School of Geography and Environmental Science
ENV2004 Urban Environments - Individual Report

Task
Write a report on urban heat islands and mitigation strategies for a mid-sized city.

Your report must include:
- an introduction to the urban heat island effect;
- an explanation of the main causes of urban heat islands;
- at least three mitigation strategies, each with an evaluation of its strengths and limitations;
- a conclusion; and
- a reference list.

Optional elements
You may include an appendix with supporting data if you wish.
Diagrams are optional.

Submit your report via the module VLE by the published deadline.
