Northbridge University - School of Nursing and Midwifery
NUR2001 Professional Practice 2 - Placement Reflection Brief

Task
Write a reflective account of a significant learning event from your most recent clinical placement.

Requirements
1. Length: 800 words (+/-10%), excluding the reference list.
2. Structure your reflection using Gibbs' reflective cycle (1988), covering all six stages: description, feelings, evaluation, analysis, conclusion and action plan.
3. Write in the first person.
4. Support your analysis with at least 3 sources (e.g. professional guidance, textbooks or journal articles).
5. Use Harvard referencing.

Confidentiality
You must maintain confidentiality in line with the NMC Code. Do not name or include any information that could identify patients, relatives, staff or placement areas.

Submit as a single document via the module VLE.
