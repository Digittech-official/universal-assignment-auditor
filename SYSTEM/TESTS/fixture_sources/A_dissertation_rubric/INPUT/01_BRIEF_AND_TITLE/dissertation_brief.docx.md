# Northbridge University
## MSc Business Management – Module BUS7021: Mini-Dissertation Brief
Academic year 2025/26. Module leader: Business School, Northbridge University.
## Dissertation Title
The Effect of Flexible Working Arrangements on Employee Retention in UK Small and Medium-sized Enterprises
## Purpose of the Assessment
This mini-dissertation assesses your ability to design and report a small-scale piece of business research, to engage critically with academic literature, and to draw practical conclusions for managers. It is worth 100% of the module mark.
## Assessment Requirements
You must follow all of the numbered instructions below. Failure to meet a requirement will be reflected in the mark awarded under the relevant rubric criterion.
1. Word count: the mini-dissertation must be 2,000 words (±10%), excluding the reference list and appendices. Tables are included in the word count.
2. Structure: the mini-dissertation must contain the following sections, in this order: Abstract; Introduction; Literature Review; Methodology; Findings; Discussion; Conclusion and Recommendations; References; Appendices.
3. Abstract: the abstract must not exceed 150 words.
4. Introduction: the introduction must state one research aim and three research objectives.
5. Research questions: the research questions must align with the stated objectives.
6. Methodology: the methodology must include a clearly headed section on ethical considerations (for example informed consent, anonymity, confidentiality, data storage and the right to withdraw).
7. Referencing: you must use the Harvard referencing style consistently for in-text citations and the reference list.
8. Sources: you must use a minimum of 10 academic sources (peer-reviewed journal articles or scholarly books). Every in-text citation must appear in the reference list and every reference-list entry must be cited in the text.
9. Findings: you must include at least one table presenting your findings.
10. Recommendations: your recommendations must be practical and actionable for SME managers.
## Submission
Submit a single Word document (.docx) via the module VLE by the published deadline. Your work will be marked against the BUS7021 marking rubric provided separately.
