# Northbridge University – BUS7021 Mini-Dissertation Marking Rubric
## Criteria and Maximum Marks
| Criterion | Maximum marks |
|---|---|
| 1. Introduction, aim and objectives | 10 |
| 2. Literature review | 25 |
| 3. Methodology | 20 |
| 4. Findings and discussion | 25 |
| 5. Conclusion and recommendations | 10 |
| 6. Presentation, structure and referencing | 10 |
| Total | 100 |
## Performance Levels
Each criterion is marked using the same percentage bands. The mark for a criterion is the band percentage applied to that criterion's maximum marks (for example, a Literature review judged at 65% receives approximately 16 of 25 marks).
| Criterion | 70–100% Excellent/First | 60–69% Very good/2:1 | 50–59% Good/2:2 | 40–49% Satisfactory/Third | 0–39% Fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Introduction, aim and objectives | Compelling rationale; one precise aim and three clear, measurable objectives; research questions fully aligned with objectives. | Clear rationale; aim and three objectives well stated; research questions aligned with objectives. | Adequate rationale; aim and objectives stated but some lack precision; alignment with research questions partly evident. | Limited rationale; aim or objectives vague or incomplete; weak alignment with research questions. | No clear rationale; aim and/or objectives missing; research questions absent or unrelated. |
| Literature review | Critical, well-synthesised review of a wide range of relevant academic sources; identifies debates and gaps that justify the study. | Good critical engagement with relevant academic sources; some synthesis and identification of gaps. | Mainly descriptive review of relevant sources with limited critical evaluation. | Largely descriptive, narrow range of sources; limited relevance to the objectives. | Very limited or irrelevant literature; little or no academic sources. |
| Methodology | Research design fully justified; sampling, data collection and analysis clearly explained; thorough ethical considerations; limitations acknowledged. | Appropriate design well explained; ethical considerations addressed; most choices justified. | Design described with some justification; ethical considerations addressed briefly; some gaps in explanation. | Design described with little justification; ethical considerations superficial or incomplete. | Methodology unclear or inappropriate; ethical considerations absent. |
| Findings and discussion | Findings presented clearly with well-constructed tables; insightful discussion that relates findings to the literature and to each objective. | Findings clearly presented; discussion links findings to literature and objectives with some insight. | Findings presented adequately; discussion partly relates findings to literature; some objectives underexplored. | Findings presented with limited clarity; discussion mostly descriptive with weak links to literature. | Findings missing, unclear or unsupported; little or no discussion. |
| Conclusion and recommendations | Conclusion addresses every objective convincingly; recommendations are practical, specific and actionable for SME managers. | Conclusion addresses all objectives; recommendations practical for SME managers. | Conclusion addresses most objectives; recommendations somewhat general. | Conclusion partly addresses objectives; recommendations generic or weakly linked to findings. | Conclusion absent or unrelated to objectives; no meaningful recommendations. |
| Presentation, structure and referencing | Required structure followed exactly; within word limit; flawless Harvard referencing; in-text citations and reference list fully consistent; at least 10 academic sources. | Required structure followed; within word limit; Harvard referencing with minor errors; at least 10 academic sources. | Structure mostly followed; minor word-count or referencing inconsistencies; source requirement largely met. | Structure partly followed; noticeable referencing errors or mismatches between citations and reference list; fewer sources than required. | Structure not followed; referencing absent or seriously deficient. |
## Grade Boundaries
| Classification | Overall mark |
|---|---|
| First | 70–100 |
| 2:1 (Upper second) | 60–69 |
| 2:2 (Lower second) | 50–59 |
| Third | 40–49 |
| Fail | 0–39 |
The overall mark out of 100 is the sum of the six criterion marks.
