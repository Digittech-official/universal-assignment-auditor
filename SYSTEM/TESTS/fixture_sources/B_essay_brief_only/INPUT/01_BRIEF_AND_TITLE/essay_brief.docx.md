# Northbridge University – School of Social and Political Sciences
## POL4010 Democracy in the Digital Age – Summative Essay Brief
Module code: POL4010. Level: 4. Assessment: Summative Essay (100% of module assessment). Submission deadline: Friday of Week 11, 12:00 noon, via the module VLE page.
## Essay question
"Critically evaluate the claim that social media has done more harm than good to democratic participation."
## Requirements
Your essay must meet all of the following requirements:
Length: 1,500 words (±10%), excluding the reference list. Essays outside this range may be penalised.
Referencing: use the Harvard referencing style consistently for in-text citations and the reference list.
Sources: draw on at least 6 academic sources (peer-reviewed journal articles or scholarly books). Newspaper articles, blogs and websites may be used in addition but do not count towards the six.
Introduction: open with an introduction that contains a clear thesis statement setting out your position on the question.
Counter-arguments: you must consider counter-arguments to your position and evaluate them, not simply mention them.
Conclusion: finish with a conclusion that directly answers the question.
Format: submit your essay as a Word document (.docx).
## Guidance
The question asks you to weigh both sides of a contested claim. Strong essays will define what they mean by "democratic participation", use evidence rather than assertion, and reach a reasoned judgement. Please see the module handbook for general guidance on academic integrity and late submission.
